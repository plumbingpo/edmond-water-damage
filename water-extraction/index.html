Every water damage restoration job starts with extraction, and no other early step matters more. The faster standing water is removed from your property, the less damage occurs to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems. Every hour that water sits on surfaces, it penetrates deeper into materials, soaking through carpet into padding, wicking up drywall, saturating wood framing and seeping into subflooring. Across Edmond, Edmond Restoration handles emergency extraction with industrial portable and truck-mounted machines that can remove hundreds of gallons a minute.

Our truck-mounted extraction units are among the most powerful water removal systems available, with the suction to pull water from carpets, hard floors and structural materials far more effectively than consumer or rental equipment. Confined spaces, wall cavities and the gaps between floor layers call for different gear, so we use portable extractors, specialty tools and submersible pumps in places the truck-mounted hoses cannot get to.

There is more to a professional extraction than getting rid of the water in plain sight. Using moisture detection equipment, our IICRC-certified team finds water that has moved out of sight, whether under cabinets, beneath flooring, behind baseboards or inside walls. Specialty extraction tools remove as much moisture as possible from these hidden areas before the drying phase begins, which significantly reduces the time and equipment needed for structural drying and lowers the risk of secondary mold growth.

How to Prevent Water Extraction Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Make sure your home has working water leak sensors in critical areas
  • Keep a basic emergency water kit: old towels, a bucket and your restoration company's number
  • Regularly inspect under sinks, around toilets and near appliances for early signs of a leak
  • Install automatic water shutoff valves that detect leaks and stop the flow
  • Know where your main water shutoff valve is and label it clearly
  • Add our number, (571) 657-6630, to your cell so help is one tap away in a water emergency

How to Tell You Need Water Extraction

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Stairwells or window wells that fill up and spill water inside
  • Drips or a steady stream coming out of a ceiling, a wall or a fixture
  • Floors flooded by a fire sprinkler or suppression system going off
  • Pooled water on the carpet, or a soggy feel with every step
  • Any room where water has collected on the floor
  • A utility area, mechanical room or crawl space with water in it
  • Wet baseboards and water creeping from beneath kitchen or bath cabinets
